## Source-discipline rules (binding on every agent and every synthesis doc)

1. Every claim is tagged **VERIFIED** (primary source fetched, quoted, URL + access date) / **ESTIMATE** (method stated) / **UNVERIFIED** (flagged; excluded from decisions).
2. Traffic numbers are always estimates — order-of-magnitude only, method stated, never the sole basis for a decision.
3. Quotes are verbatim + permalinked + archived (archive.today/Wayback) at capture time.
4. No claims about competitors' internal metrics unless they published them themselves.
5. Failure cases are mandatory: ≥1 per 2 successes (Agents 1, 2, 4). Survivorship bias is the default error.
6. Every research file ends with **"So what for BTCSCAM"** + **"Open questions."**
7. Published spin-off articles inherit the trust-ladder labeling — estimates are labeled in the published piece exactly as in dossiers.

## Decision gates

- Every synthesis insight terminates in **ADOPT / REJECT / PARK** — no undecided insights.
- `synthesis/prd-delta.md` proposes diffs; **PRD.md is not edited** until Mitul approves the delta. The community build phase is blocked on that approval and nothing else.
