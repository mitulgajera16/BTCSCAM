# Phase Community — Roadmap (reshaped R3)

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · Derived from `insight-memo.md` (insight numbers cited as I-n). Replaces the PRD's original R3 sequencing pending `prd-delta.md` approval.

**The reshape in one line:** the machinery is already built (accounts, ladder, votes, desk all shipped) — what the research changes is the **activation order**: content seeds first, the feedback loop closes second, and community mechanics activate last, each stage feeding the next. "Tooling before community is empty rooms" survived contact with the evidence; "community before seeding" did not survive it either — every living platform launched pre-seeded (I-6).

## Stage A — "Paper first" (weeks 1–2): content velocity before community

- Publish guides **1+3 as a pair** (wallet-phishing recognition + hardware authenticity) inside the live Coldcard news window; then **2** (recovery-service scams) and **4** (seed storage, with the first SeedBook disclosure block in the HIBP grammar) (I-13, I-11).
- Seed **10–15 dossiers** into the registry from documented public cases — the ledger must launch "with hundreds of reports available to search" in spirit, Chainabuse-style (I-6). Draft queue from the ingest cron is the raw material; Monday Sweep is the ritual.
- **Monday Sweep becomes a published editorial artifact** (a weekly roundup page/post composed from /desk activity) — the community ritual exists as content before any user joins it (I-6).
- Sitewide: recovery-scam warning banner on every dossier; dirty-verdict abstention policy line on /check (I-9); phrases/aliases field added to the incident schema so dossiers capture the victim's search grammar (I-8).

## Stage B — "The findable check" (weeks 2–4): win the search moment

- /check upgrades to evidence-first presentation: report count, blacklist hits, domain signals, linked dossiers — never a composite score; verdict stays same-surface, zero-navigation, no account (I-7, I-12).
- Dossier SEO pass: victim-phrase-grammar titles and FAQ blocks; submit sitemaps to search consoles; distribute each new dossier where its victims are already discussing it (growth-playbook play #2, now evidence-backed by the ≥400/month unanswered firehose).
- Presence in the existing channel: answer zero-reply "is this a scam" threads in r/Scams/r/CryptoScams with genuine help + canonical dossier links — absorb the unanswered majority rather than demand venue change (05/F4). Cadence: founder-scale, ~30 min on Sweep days; never drive-by promo.

## Stage C — "The loop closes" (weeks 4–8): become the anti-black-hole

- Reporter-visible status timeline on every report: open → corroborated → verified/rejected, with dated transitions (I-5). The machinery exists (/reports/open); this stage makes state changes *legible and narrated*.
- Optional reply-channel field on /report ("we commonly have questions" — BleepingComputer posture); zero-PII submission stays the default and the form says so (04 decisions 3/5).
- First outcome counter: report → registrar/host abuse escalation for confirmed phishing domains; publish "takedowns confirmed" the day the first one lands (I-5). This is the compensation loop hunters work for.
- Publish the trust-state promotion rubric as a public page (HIBP's five questions precedent, I-2).

## Stage D — "Watchmen live" (weeks 8–12): activate the ladder

Gate: Stages A–C shipped, safelist + dispute/right-of-reply lane live (I-10) — abuse tooling precedes promotion mechanics, per Tiger #1.

- Recruit the first 5–10 contributors **by personal invitation from where they already hunt** (Bitcointalk Scam Accusations veterans, r/CryptoScams regulars) with the investigator-identity pitch: named credit, visible outcomes, dense tools (I-3, I-4).
- Verify-votes become reputation-bearing: corroborations later overturned cost ladder standing; the math is simple and published (I-2).
- Corroborators are named on dossier pages ("Corroborated by …") — real credit, pseudonymous by default, Signal-style per-context exposure (04 decision 5).
- Ladder copy frames every tier as protecting the next person; the Reporter tier's job is shame-conversion (I-4).

## Funnel wiring (runs through all stages, both products equally)

Per the 02 spec table: SeedBook disclosure block ships with guide 4 (Stage A); /check clean-verdict SeedBook line ships in Stage B; dirty verdict sells nothing, ever, and says so (I-9, I-11). Bitwill's inheritance guide is deliberately batch-2 — after the disclosure grammar is proven on SeedBook links — then dossier "WHAT TO DO NOW" blocks on inheritance-relevant incidents may carry the disclosed Bitwill line. Newsletter revival (one labeled house slot, alternating products) is a Stage C/D decision, not before.
