# The State of Scam Reporting 2026 — brief

**Date:** 2026-08-11 · Source for the shareable artifact page; findings from `01`, `02`, `03` (methods and full evidence there). All numbers below carry their method; estimates are labeled.

## Three headline findings

1. **The unanswered firehose.** ≥400 "is this a scam?" posts per month in r/Scams alone (8-day archive sample, limit-capped — floor, not ceiling); in our samples the majority received zero replies. The moment of highest intent is served by silence, SEO spam, and paid ads for phishing sites.
2. **The black hole.** The loudest cross-venue complaint about official channels: file with IC3/FTC → a confirmation number → silence, forever. The FBI receives ~2,400 scam reports per day (NPR, via community citation). Wins exist but are invisible — they never feed back into public confidence.
3. **The colonization.** Every surface where victims gather is farmed by "recovery" scammers within hours — including the review sections of the scam-checker sites themselves. The checker most victims find first (ScamAdviser) is distrusted with receipts in both directions: confirmed scams scored 91/100 "high trust," legitimate small businesses flagged.

## What the landscape looks like (12 platforms examined)

- Only one platform on the internet runs genuine community verification (Bitcointalk's reputation-staked trust system). Everyone else: staff verdicts, a single accountable human, or a no-publication government sink.
- No platform anywhere runs a contributor status ladder. Reporter incentives are startlingly thin: closure narratives, chosen-name attribution, karma. The people doing the best volunteer scam-hunting work are paid in takedown confirmations and thanks.
- Every surviving platform launched pre-seeded (partners, existing corpora, an editor's content). Zero started as an empty submission form.
- Every surviving platform has a host organism (enterprise parent, tax funding, newsroom, one-person-with-revenue). All three dead databases examined were unfunded volunteer projects.
- The category leader's economics: Chainabuse earns $0 directly — it is a free public good feeding TRM Labs' enterprise business ($218M+ raised, $1B valuation, Feb 2026). And victims largely can't find it; a phishing clone intercepted its brand within weeks.

## What no one has built (the gap BTCSCAM fills)

Fast AND verified AND readable — plus three structural absences nobody occupies: **visible report status** (the anti-black-hole), **a named contributor ladder** (status, not money — matching the motivation that actually exists: "so no one else falls for it"), and **evidence-first verdicts** (what the evidence shows, never a single trust score).
